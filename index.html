Protect lives — call for urgent action

Communities across Nigeria are experiencing deadly attacks that threaten innocent lives and religious freedom. This petition asks local authorities, national leaders, and the international community to take immediate steps to protect civilians, investigate abuses, and provide humanitarian support.

We demand:

  • Transparent and independent investigations into attacks on communities.
  • Improved security measures and protection for vulnerable populations.
  • Immediate humanitarian assistance and resettlement support for displaced families.
  • International cooperation to prevent further violence and ensure accountability.
